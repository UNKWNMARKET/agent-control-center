To record it in your own voice: press 🎙 Read it yourself. The narration jumps to teleprompter size, every scene gets 30% longer so you are not rushed, and the amber bar under the timeline shows how much of the current line is left. Start your screen recorder (Windows: ⊞ + Alt + R), press play, wait for the 3‑2‑1, and read. Your voice beats any computer voice for this.
To use the built-in voice instead: turn on 🔊 Narration and pick the best voice your computer has from the dropdown. How good that is depends entirely on which voices are installed on the machine playing it.
