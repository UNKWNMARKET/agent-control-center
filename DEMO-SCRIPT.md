# Demo script — Agent Control Center

About three minutes. Sign in to the demo workspace first, so nothing real is
on screen. Everything below is already in there; you are not typing anything
you have to invent.

The one rule: **slow down on the sold flow.** That is the part no other CRM
does, and it is the part that sells this.

---

### 0:00 — Dashboard

> "This is what an agent sees when they open up in the morning. New leads,
> what's being worked, what sold. Nothing to configure — it's just the
> state of the agency."

Hover a tile so the card lifts. Click **New this week**.

---

### 0:20 — Leads

> "Every lead that comes in from EverQuote, SmartFinancial, your website —
> they all land here automatically. You're not copying anything out of an
> email."

Point at the green and red dots.

> "That dot is do-not-call. It's checked before anybody picks up the phone,
> so nobody on your team calls a number they shouldn't."

Click **Robert Okafor**.

---

### 0:45 — A lead, up close

> "Here's the whole file on one screen."

Scroll to the property panel.

> "This ran by itself the second the lead arrived — straight off the Florida
> tax roll. He owns it, it's worth $735,000, built in 2012. And look at this
> line: no homestead exemption on file. He bought in 2023 and probably never
> filed. That's a conversation your competitor isn't having."

Point at the phone number.

> "Click the number, it dials through your phone system. Click the message
> icon, it texts. Whatever system you already use — RingCentral, Dialpad,
> Zoom — you pick it once in settings and it just works."

Scroll the timeline.

> "Every call, text, note and status change, in order, with who did it."

---

### 1:20 — Working it

Add a quick note. Set a follow-up for tomorrow.

> "Set a follow-up and it comes back to you on the dashboard. Nothing falls
> through."

---

### 1:40 — Marking it sold (slow down here)

Open the status dropdown and pick **Sold**.

> "Now watch this. This is the part I built because I kept losing business
> to missing paperwork."

Walk the questions out loud as they appear:

> "What did you sell? AAA. Auto or package? Package. Right away it wants the
> VIN checks — I can't get past this screen without them. Did you declare
> prior auto? Yes — now it wants the prior auto dec page. Did you give the
> wind mitigation discount? Yes — now it wants the wind mit. And proof of
> prior home insurance, every single time."

Try to submit with something missing so the block shows.

> "It will not let me mark this sold. Not a reminder, not a warning — it
> won't do it. So underwriting never comes back three weeks later asking
> for a document nobody kept."

Then the policy number field.

> "And it always asks for the policy number, so you can search by it later."

---

### 2:20 — Book of business

Go to **Book of business**. Show the three tabs.

> "Everything in force, with the written premium. Renewal shows anything
> expiring in the next 45 days — that's Dale Whitcomb, December tenth, so
> somebody's requoting him this week. Cancelled keeps the history with the
> reason."

> "And if you're bringing a book with you, you can add existing customers
> straight in."

---

### 2:40 — Running the agency

Click through **Team**, then **Integrations**, then **Settings**.

> "You invite your own staff — they set their own passwords, you never
> handle them. Each lead source gets its own connection. And your phone
> system is one dropdown."

> "Your data is yours. No other agency can see a single record of it, ever."

---

### 2:55 — Close

> "That's it. Monthly, no contract, and I set the whole thing up for you."

---

## Notes for recording

- Full screen, browser chrome hidden if you can (F11).
- Zoom the browser to about 110% — it reads better on a phone.
- Don't narrate the mouse ("now I'm clicking…"). Say what it *means*.
- If you fluff a line, pause two seconds and say it again. Easier to cut
  than to re-record.
- The demo workspace resets to nothing if you ask me to reseed it, so feel
  free to click anything, including marking something sold.
